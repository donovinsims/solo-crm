# Relay Brand Spec

## Primary Accent
- **Hex (light)**: #E06C4A
- **Hex (dark)**: #E88D6A
- **Name**: Terracotta
- **Rationale**: Warm, earthy, professional — evokes the brick storefronts of Roscoe/Rockford, approachable for local business owners, distinct from generic SaaS blue

## Neutral Palette (warm undertones)
- Background (light): #FAFAF8 | (dark): #1A1A18
- Surface (light): #FFFFFF | (dark): #242422
- Text Primary (light): #1A1A1A | (dark): #FAFAF8
- Text Secondary (light): #6B6B6B | (dark): #A0A0A0
- Text Tertiary (light): #9B9B9B | (dark): #6B6B6B
- Border (light): #E8E8E0 | (dark): #3A3A36

## Typography
- Display: New York (serif) — headings, client names
- Body: SF Pro — all UI text
- Scale: Display 34pt → Title 28pt → Headline 20pt → Body 17pt → Caption 13pt → Caption2 11pt

## Spacing
- 8pt grid: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48

## Corner Radius
- 8, 12, 16, 28
